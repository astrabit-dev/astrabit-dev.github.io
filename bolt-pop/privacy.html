Bolt Pop: Screw Jam — Privacy Policy

Package com.astrabit.boltpop · Astrabit · Last updated 28 November 2026

What the game stores

Bolt Pop keeps your progress, coins, unlocked themes, awards, daily streak and setting switches in the app's private storage on your device. Nothing is sent to a server: the game has no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync and no analytics SDK. Clearing the app's data, or using Reset progress in Settings, erases it.

Advertising

The game shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect a device advertising identifier, coarse device and network information, and ad interaction data in order to serve and measure ads. The Google User Messaging Platform asks for your consent where the law requires it, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy policy → Ad privacy options inside the game. See Google's advertising policies for how Google uses this data.

Children

Bolt Pop is a general-audience puzzle game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

Internet and network state are used only to load ads. Vibration is used for optional haptic feedback you can turn off in Settings. The in-app web view is locked to the bundled game files and cannot load remote pages.
