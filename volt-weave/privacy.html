Privacy Policy — Volt Weave: Circuit Puzzle

Package com.astrabit.voltweave · Developer Astrabit · Last updated 27 November 2026

Volt Weave: Circuit Puzzle is a single-player puzzle game published by Astrabit. This page explains exactly what the app stores, what it does not collect, and how advertising works.

1. What stays on your device

Your progress is stored only inside the app, in the WebView's localStorage under the key voltweave.save.v1. That includes cleared stages, stars, turns, coins, boosters, unlocked skins, your check-in streak, daily quests, awards and your sound, music, vibration and language settings.

There is no account and no sign-in. Nothing you do in the game is uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes the save file. The Android manifest sets android:allowBackup="false", so the save is not copied into a cloud backup either.

2. Fully offline game

The game itself ships inside the app. The Android WebView is configured with blockNetworkLoads=true and only ever loads file:///android_asset/…, so the game never fetches scripts, fonts or images from the internet. All 180 stages and the daily circuits are generated offline and included in the bundle.

3. Advertising

Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, Google may process a device advertising identifier, IP address, and coarse device information. We do not receive your name, email or phone number, and we do not combine ad data with your game progress.

Before any ad request, consent is collected through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. You can review or change your choices at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy settings. Where consent is declined, only non-personalised ads are requested.

Ads are never shown during a puzzle. A banner is hidden while you play, and an interstitial can appear only after you clear a stage, at most once every 75 seconds. A rewarded ad is optional and only grants its booster after the ad's completion callback.

4. Permissions

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, AD_ID — used only by the advertising SDK.
  • VIBRATE — short haptic feedback when a tile turns; it can be switched off in Settings.

The app does not request contacts, photos, files, location, microphone or camera access.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. It contains no chat, no user-generated content and no social features.

6. Your choices

You can reset all local progress at any time in Settings → Reset progress, and you can turn sound, music or vibration off individually. Because no personal data is stored on our side, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
