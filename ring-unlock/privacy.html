Privacy Policy

Ring Unlock: Orbit Twist · com.astrabit.ringunlock · developed by Astrabit · last updated 25 November 2026

1. What this game stores

Ring Unlock stores only your own progress: coins, stars per stage, which ring skins you own, your login streak, daily quests, achievement flags and your sound, music, haptics and language settings. This data is written to the device's own localStorage under the key ringunlock.save.v1. It never leaves the device, and Astrabit has no way to read it.

2. No account, no server, no cloud

There is no sign-up, no login, no cloud save and no analytics SDK in this game. Nothing you do is sent to a server owned by Astrabit. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, the save is deleted with it, and that deletion cannot be undone.

3. Advertising

Advertising is provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). The game shows a banner on menus, an interstitial after some cleared stages, and an optional rewarded ad when you ask for a booster. Google may collect and use an advertising identifier, device model, coarse location derived from IP address and ad interaction data in order to serve and measure advertising. This processing is described in Google's own privacy policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Before any ad request, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent SDK. Where consent is required, a form appears first and no ad is requested until you answer. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time from Settings → Ad consent inside the game, which reopens the same UMP form.

4. Permissions

The app declares the INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and Google's AD_ID permission, all of which exist only for advertising. The game itself is fully offline: it is loaded from inside the app package, the WebView is configured with blockNetworkLoads=true, and no remote page, image, font or script is ever loaded into the play area. No camera, microphone, contacts, storage or location permission is requested.

5. Children

Ring Unlock is not directed at children under 13. Astrabit does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the ad system, contact the address below and we will help you exercise Google's ad settings for that device.

6. Data sharing and retention

Astrabit does not sell, trade or share data with anyone. The only third party involved is Google's ad stack described above. Save data stays on your device for as long as you keep the game installed; ad-related data is retained by Google under its own policies.

7. Your choices

  • Settings → Ad consent: change or withdraw ad consent (UMP form).
  • Settings → Reset all progress: erase the local save immediately.
  • Uninstalling the app removes every file the game created.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version is published at this address and the date above is revised. Material changes will also be noted in the app's Play listing.
