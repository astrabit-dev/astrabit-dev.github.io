Privacy Policy

Color Gate Jam: Block Puzzle · package com.astrabit.colorgatejam
Effective 25 November 2026 · Version 1.0 · Last updated 25 November 2026

English · 한국어

The short version. Color Gate Jam is an offline puzzle game. We run no server, there is no account, and we do not collect or receive personal information about you. Your progress, coins and settings are stored only on your own device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may process device and advertising data for that purpose; you can change your advertising consent at any time in the app's settings.

1. Who we are

Color Gate Jam: Block Puzzle is developed and published by Astrabit ("we", "us"). For anything in this policy, write to codepanda2020@gmail.com. This policy covers the Android application com.astrabit.colorgatejam.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. We have no backend, no analytics service of our own, no crash-reporting service of our own, and no user accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location, and the app does not request permission for any of them.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps a save file in the app's local storage (localStorage, key blockgate.save.v1) so it can resume where you left off. It contains:

Stored on the deviceWhy
Level progress, stars, best move countsTo unlock levels and show your record
Coins, boosters, owned themesTo keep purchases and rewards
Settings: language, sound, music, vibration, themeTo remember your choices
Daily reward day, streak, quest date, daily-challenge resultTo run the daily features
A save-format version number and creation timeTo migrate old saves safely

This data never leaves the device through us. It is not linked to any identifier that could reach us, and we cannot read it. Android's cloud backup and device-transfer for this app are disabled, so the save is not copied to Google's servers by the operating system.

Deleting your data

Settings → Reset progress deletes the save inside the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes the save permanently. Because nothing is stored on our side, there is no copy we could restore.

3. Advertising

Ads keep the game going. They are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC and its affiliates, using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK for consent. Ads are requested only after the consent flow has been resolved.

For ad delivery and measurement, Google may process data such as:

  • advertising ID and other device identifiers, and app-set or installer information;
  • IP address and a coarse location derived from it;
  • which ads were shown, clicked or dismissed, and app interaction events used for measurement;
  • device type, operating system version, language and general diagnostics.

We do not receive this data as individuals; Google reports aggregate performance (for example, impression and revenue totals) to us.

Your choices

  • In the app: Settings → Ad privacy settings opens the Google consent form, where you can accept or refuse personalized advertising, or withdraw consent.
  • On your device: Android settings → Privacy → Ads lets you delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • Where consent is required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), ads that rely on personal data are shown only if you allow it; otherwise you get non-personalized ads, which still use limited data such as coarse location and contextual signals.

Google's own practices are described in the Google Privacy & Terms and Google Privacy Policy. Ads need a network connection; the puzzle itself does not.

4. Permissions and network use

  • Internet — used only for ad requests. The game page itself is loaded from inside the app, and the app blocks web content from loading anything over the network, so game files are never fetched remotely.
  • Vibration — haptic feedback, which you can switch off in settings.

No other Android permission is requested.

5. Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies: personalized advertising and related measurement are based on your consent, which you may withdraw at any time in the app's ad privacy settings. Non-personalized advertising relies on our legitimate interest in funding a game we offer without charge, balanced against the limited data involved. We process no other personal data.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because we hold no personal data about you, most requests end at your device: clearing app data or uninstalling removes everything we would otherwise control. Requests about advertising data held by Google can be made through the Google tools linked above, or you can write to us at codepanda2020@gmail.com and we will help where we can.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising beyond what the ad consent you gave allows.

7. Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will act on it; in practice there is nothing stored on our side to delete, and removing the app removes the local save.

8. Third parties

The only third-party service in the app is Google AdMob with the UMP consent SDK, provided by Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA), which may process data outside your country under its own safeguards. We use no other SDKs, no social login, no game services and no attribution or analytics providers.

9. Security

Because the app sends us nothing, there is no account or database of yours to breach. The save file sits in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox and your device's lock screen. Ads are served over HTTPS by Google.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app starts doing something new with data — for example adding cloud saves or a new ad format — this policy will be updated before that version ships, with a new date and version number at the top. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the updated policy.
