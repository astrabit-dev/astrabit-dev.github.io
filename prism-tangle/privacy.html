Privacy Policy — Prism Tangle: Glow Knot

Package com.astrabit.prismtangle · Developer Astrabit · Effective 27 November 2026

Prism Tangle is a single-player puzzle game published by Astrabit. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no cloud sync. This page explains exactly what the app does with data.

1. Data the game stores

Your progress (cleared stages, stars, coins, unlocked themes, achievements, daily streak) and your settings (language, sound, music, vibration) are stored in the app's local storage on your device. They never leave the device, are not readable by us, and are removed when you reset progress in Settings or uninstall the app.

The app does not request your name, email address, contacts, photos, files, precise location, camera, microphone or health data, and does not collect analytics or crash reports.

2. Advertising

The app shows advertising supplied by Google AdMob (banner, interstitial after a stage clear, and an optional rewarded video that grants a booster). To serve and measure ads, Google may access a device advertising identifier and technical information such as device type, IP address and general region. We do not receive personal data from Google; we only see aggregate earnings reports.

Where required, a consent form provided by Google's User Messaging Platform appears before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy options button in the app's privacy dialog. See Google's partner sites policy for how Google uses this information.

3. Network use

The game itself runs fully offline: all levels, fonts, sounds and graphics ship inside the app. Network access is used only by the advertising SDK described above.

4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Your choices

  • Turn off advertising consent in Privacy options where the consent form is offered.
  • Erase all local data with Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
